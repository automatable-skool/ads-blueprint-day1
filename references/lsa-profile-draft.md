## 5. Service area - wide GTA

**Your choice:** set it broad. Google recommends this.

**Starting area:** Toronto including East York, North York, Etobicoke and Scarborough, plus the inner GTA of Mississauga, Vaughan, Markham and Richmond Hill, plus the outer GTA of Brampton, Oakville, Pickering and Ajax.

## 6. Business hours - open 24/7

**Your choice:** set hours to Open 24 hours, 7 days, in both the Business Profile and LSA.

**The catch:** 24/7 only helps if a real person answers every call, day or night. Missed and voicemail calls hurt your responsiveness ranking, and they hurt more when you advertise round-the-clock.

If after-hours coverage is shaky, use an answering service or list only the hours you will truly pick up. Also make sure "Emergency plumbing" is turned on in your job types above.

## 8. Verification documents - the bottleneck, submit these first

Three checks, typically about three to four weeks, sometimes two to five or longer. Ads stay paused until the Google Verified badge clears, and a bigger budget does NOT speed it up.

1. **Licence check** - your required plumbing and trade licences. **[GATHER]**
2. **Insurance check** - a certificate of insurance for general liability, around $1M commonly cited. It must be **valid for at least 14 days** after you submit it. **[GATHER]**
3. **Background check** - the owner plus all field workers, which applies in Canada. Plumbing counts as an urgent trade and may get extra screening. **[START EARLY for all 3 workers]**

## After you are live - the two levers that matter most

**Speed to lead.** Answer every call live, targeting under five minutes, and never let an LSA call hit voicemail. Reply to messages immediately. Responsiveness is a ranking factor and missed calls hurt you.

**Reviews.** Ask every happy customer within 24 hours using a one-tap Google review link, aim for a steady drip rather than a burst, and respond to all of them.
