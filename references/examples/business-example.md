## What we do

- Residential plumbing company - repairs, installations, drains, and emergency service.
- Residential customers only, no commercial work.
- Locally owned and family-run since 2012.
- How a job runs: book by phone, text or online, then an in-person diagnosis, then an upfront flat-rate quote before any work begins, with a live technician tracking link on the way.

## Services offered

- Leak detection and repair, hidden and visible leaks
- Drain cleaning and unclogging - sinks, tubs, toilets, main lines
- Camera and video drain inspections
- Water heater installation, repair and replacement, tank and tankless
- Fixtures and faucets - toilet, faucet and fixture repair and installation
- Sump pump installation, repair, and battery backup systems
- Burst pipe and frozen pipe repair
- Repiping in copper and PEX
- Backwater valve installation for basement flood prevention
- Emergency repairs, same-day availability, 24/7 emergency line

## Who we serve

- Homeowners, the primary customer - detached, semi and townhouse
- Condo residents
- Landlords and rental property owners, including multi-unit portfolios
- Residential only. No commercial, industrial, or new-construction work.

## Where we work

**Core area:** Toronto, including East York, North York, Etobicoke and Scarborough.

**Also served:** Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham.

**Travel limit:** up to 45 minutes from the Toronto shop. Anything further is declined or referred out.

**Shop address:** 111 Main Street, Toronto, ON.

## What we don't do - this is the starting negative keyword list

- Commercial or industrial plumbing
- New-construction plumbing and builder contracts
- Septic tanks and wells
- Gas line work - not TSSA gas-licensed, we refer it out
- HVAC, furnaces, boilers, radiant heating
- Pools, hot tubs, irrigation, sprinkler systems
- Appliance repair - we connect dishwashers and washing machines, we don't fix them
- Jobs outside the 45-minute radius, so Hamilton, Oshawa and Barrie are out
- Do-it-yourself support calls and parts-only sales

## Company story

- Founded in 2012 by brothers Dan and Mike Abcott, which is where "ABC" comes from.
- Family-owned and operated for 14 years.
- Team of 9: 6 licensed plumbers, 2 apprentices, 1 dispatcher.
- Started as a one-van operation in East York, now runs 5 service vans across the GTA.
- 12,000+ residential jobs completed since 2012.
