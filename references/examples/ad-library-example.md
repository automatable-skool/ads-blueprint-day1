# Ad library - ABC Company

Every headline and description that survived the cull, for the three biggest clusters: Generic Plumber, Urgent Plumber, Drain Cleaning.
Built 7 August 2026, culled the same day.
Next: build the ads. Each ad group needs 3 pinned headlines, 12 unpinned headlines and 4 descriptions.

## Rules every line here already follows

**Never write the star symbol.** Google's editorial review rejects it outright. Spell out "stars" instead - "4.9 Stars, 287 reviews".

**No exclamation marks and no phone numbers.** Both are automatic disapprovals.

**The 60-minute response claim is Toronto only.** Any line using it is marked "Toronto only" and must never run in another city's ad group.

**No free-estimate offers in the Urgent pool.** Emergency callers are not shopping for quotes.

**Every claim traces back to proof.md.** If it is not in that file, it does not go in an ad.

**Headlines stay at 30 characters or under. Descriptions stay at 90 characters or under.**

## How the pinned keyword lines work

The first three lines in each pool are the pinned position-1 slots. They exist to match the search term and lift Quality Score, not to persuade, so they were never scored against the persuasion bar. Every other line was.

The last of the three uses dynamic keyword insertion, written as `{Keyword:...}` - Google swaps in the customer's actual search when it fits, and falls back to the written phrase when it does not.

## Lines you can unlock by adding proof

These seven were killed only because proof.md cannot back them. Add the underlying fact to proof.md and they go straight into the library:

- Boot covers at the door
- We leave it cleaner
- Tidy work, tidier exit
- The owners still answer
- Same week, guaranteed slot
- No subcontractors
- Warranty most won't match

## The pattern worth remembering

Almost nothing died for being false. Lines died for being interchangeable, meaning a near-duplicate of a sharper phrasing already in the pool, or for being generic - the "nice-sounding service words" sections were wiped out entirely.

What survived is exactly the proof-backed numbers and the gap-owning mechanics the competitor scan said this market is missing.
