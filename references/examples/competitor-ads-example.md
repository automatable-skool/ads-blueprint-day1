# Competitor ads - Toronto plumbing market

What every competitor is claiming in their Google ads, which claims nobody has taken, and the best lines in the market scored for calibration.
Scouted 7 August 2026, covering the three biggest clusters: Generic Plumber, Urgent Plumber, Drain Cleaning.
Next: write the ad library against the gaps below - they are the open lanes.

## Where this came from

16 competitors were scouted from the live Google results for the head terms - plumber toronto, plumber mississauga, plumber hamilton, emergency plumber toronto, drain cleaning toronto.

Their ads were pulled from Google's Ads Transparency Centre: 357 creatives in total, filtered down to text ads still running in July and August 2026, deduplicated, and capped at 12 per advertiser. That left **79 live ads across 9 advertisers**.

**Currently running ads:** Anta Plumbing, Birnie & Sons, GTA Restoration, Master Drain, Mr. Rooter, New Canadian Drain & Plumbing, Priority Plumbing, Reliance Home Comfort, Roto-Rooter.

**Scouted but running nothing right now:** Mister Plumber, Brothers Plumbing, Plumber To Your Door, Absolute Draining & Plumbing and Dr. Pipe have no ads at all. Crystal Drain has 17 ads on file and The Great Plumbing Co. has 8, but neither has shown one since before July 2026.

Those dormant advertisers still rank in the normal Google results, so their website claims are counted below and marked "from their website" rather than from an ad.

Mr. Rooter and Roto-Rooter ads were sampled from their Edmonton, Hamilton and Vancouver franchises. The claims are network-wide templates, so they are treated as what those brands would run in Toronto.

## Table stakes - what almost everyone claims

An ad missing these is under-equipped on every search result page.

**24/7, 24-hour, around-the-clock.** Speed and availability. 13 of the 16 competitors. Anta, Birnie, Mr. Rooter, Roto-Rooter, Reliance, New Canadian, GTA Restoration, Priority, Master Drain, plus Mister Plumber, Plumber To Your Door, Brothers and Great Plumbing Co. from their websites.

**Emergency service.** Speed and urgency. 10 of 16. Anta, New Canadian, Roto-Rooter, GTA Restoration, Master Drain, Priority, plus Brothers, Mister Plumber, Plumber To Your Door and Great Plumbing Co. from their websites.

**Licensed, usually insured too.** Trust. 6 of 16. Anta, Reliance, Priority, New Canadian, Mr. Rooter, plus Mister Plumber from their website.

**Years in business.** Trust. 5 of 16. "Trusted 100 Years", "Since 1935", "20+ Years". Birnie, Roto-Rooter, Priority, GTA Restoration, plus Brothers with "since 1969" from their website.

**A dollar-off coupon.** Offer. 4 of 16. "$50 Off Drain Cleaning", "CA$100 off", "$55 or $75 Off Today", 10% for seniors. Birnie, Mr. Rooter, Roto-Rooter, New Canadian.

**Same-day service.** Speed. 4 of 16. Priority, New Canadian, Roto-Rooter, plus Plumber To Your Door from their website.

**Star rating and review count inside the ad.** Trust and social proof. 4 of 16. Anta, New Canadian, Roto-Rooter, Reliance.

Two notes on this list. Review stars come from Local Services listings and seller ratings, which is an account setup problem, not a copywriting one. Heritage cannot be faked - counter it with a different kind of trust proof instead.

## Claims two or three competitors own

**Free estimate or free quote.** Offer, light risk reversal. 3 competitors: Priority, New Canadian, Mr. Rooter.

**Flat-rate, no hidden fees, upfront pricing.** Price. 2 competitors: Reliance and Mr. Rooter.

**BBB A+ rated.** Trust. 2 competitors: Anta and Birnie.

**An arrival-time promise.** Speed. 2 competitors: Anta with "At Your Door Within An Hour" and GTA Restoration with "60 minutes or less".

**No job too big, too small or too tough.** Capability. 2 competitors: Anta and Roto-Rooter.

## Claims exactly one competitor owns

**No overtime charges.** Price. Mr. Rooter only.

**No trip charge.** Price. Roto-Rooter only.

**Government rebate, "Get Up to $3,400 Rebate".** Offer. New Canadian only, on backwater valves.

**"Voted #1 GTA Plumbers" and a HomeStars award.** Trust through superlative. New Canadian only.

**100% Canadian, locally owned.** Identity. New Canadian only.

**Camera or video inspection.** Method, drain jobs. Anta only.

**"Friendly plumbers".** Service feel. Birnie only.

**"100% guaranteed", with nothing behind it.** Risk reversal. Reliance only.

## The gaps - angles nobody is running

This is the whole point of the scan. Seven open lanes.

**1. Real risk reversal. Nobody.** Not one advertiser says "fixed right or you don't pay", offers money back, or names a workmanship warranty like "1-year labour warranty on every drain job". Reliance's vague "100% guaranteed" is the only whisper of it. This is the biggest open lane on the board.

**2. An actual price in the ad. Nobody.** Coupons are everywhere, but no one anchors a real number - "Drain snaking from $XX", or "Upfront quote before we start, approved by you". The whole market says "affordable" and never proves it.

If you use a price, it has to be live on the landing page and in proof.md.

**3. A specific response time in Toronto copy. Almost nobody.** Only Anta with "within an hour", and GTA Restoration, which is a restoration company rather than a plumber. The planned "On Our Way in 30 Min, 24/7" headline has no direct competition, provided proof.md can back the number.

**4. Disclosing the call-out fee. One or two franchise ads only.** "No overtime charge" and "no trip charge" each appear exactly once, and neither comes from a Toronto independent. Pairing "no call-out fee" with "upfront price" is completely unclaimed locally.

**5. Online booking or text-to-book. Nobody.** Every single ad's call to action is "Call". "Book online in 60 seconds" or "see your plumber's ETA" is unowned.

**6. Respect-your-home proof. Nobody.** Background-checked technicians, uniforms, floor protection, a clean-up promise - absent from all 79 ads.

**7. Bundling the drain camera. Nobody.** No one offers "free camera inspection with every drain cleaning", even though Anta sells the inspection separately. Strong differentiator for the drain cluster.

## Do not copy these - compliance

**GTA Restoration puts a phone number in the ad headline.** That is an automatic disapproval for us.

**New Canadian's "Voted #1" needs third-party verification visible on the page.** Without that it is an unprovable superlative.

**Every dollar-off coupon has to exist live on the landing page.** Google checks.

## Swipe file - the best lines in the market

Benchmarks only. **We never copy these.** Each is scored out of 10 on the same rubric the ad library uses: specific, differentiated, instantly clear, proof-backed, tight, true to its angle.

One adjustment for competitors: "proof-backed" is judged against their own verifiable proof - heritage, review counts, real programs - not our proof.md. "Differentiated" is judged against the claim list above.

### Headlines

**"Trusted Plumbers For 100 Years"** - Birnie, trust angle, scores 9 of 10.
A number nobody else can say, instantly understood, backed by a real 1920s founding. The ceiling for a trust line.

**"Trusted Since 1935"** - Roto-Rooter, trust angle, scores 9 of 10.
The same play compressed under 20 characters. The year does the work of a paragraph.

**"No Overtime Charge"** - Mr. Rooter, price angle, scores 8 of 10.
Concrete, tight, and it answers the 2am fear directly: what is this going to cost me tonight? Sole owner of the claim.

**"No Trip Charge"** - Roto-Rooter, price angle, scores 8 of 10.
Fourteen characters that remove the exact fee emergency callers dread. Unique in this market.

**"Get Up to $3,400 Rebate"** - New Canadian, offer angle, scores 8 of 10.
The only real dollar figure in any ad, and borrowing a government program's authority makes it credible.

**"Tuesday Special: $25 Off"** - Birnie, offer angle, scores 8 of 10.
Day-of-week scarcity. The only time-boxed offer running, and it makes the coupon feel live rather than permanent.

**"10% off Seniors Discount"** - New Canadian, offer angle, scores 8 of 10.
The only offer aimed at a segment. The audience self-identifies before clicking, which buys cheap relevance.

**"At Your Door Within An Hour."** - Anta, speed angle, scores 7 of 10.
The market's only arrival-time promise. Specific and native to how an urgent searcher thinks. Loses points only because no proof is visible.

**"$50 Off Drain Cleaning"** - Birnie, offer angle, scores 7 of 10.
A number plus a named service in six words. This is the template that makes their whole city-by-city campaign work.

**"Blocked Drains Hamilton - $50 Coupon"** - Birnie, offer plus keyword, scores 7 of 10.
Symptom keyword, city and offer stacked into one slot. A lesson in stacking, though "coupon" cheapens the brand a notch.

**"New Toilet Installation - Same Day Service, Call Now"** - New Canadian, speed angle, scores 7 of 10.
Service, timeframe and call to action with zero wasted words. Same-day is exactly what that searcher is deciding on.

**"Voted #1 GTA Plumbers"** - New Canadian, trust through superlative, scores 6 of 10.
Punchy and differentiated, but an unverified superlative. Never say this - it is an automatic compliance kill for us.

**"Same Day Service - Affordable Plumbing Care"** - Roto-Rooter, speed and price, scores 6 of 10.
A real timeframe dragged down by "affordable", the word every ad reaches for instead of a number.

**"Roto-Rooter Plumbing - Available 24/7"** - Roto-Rooter, speed angle, scores 5 of 10.
Clean writing, but thirteen competitors say the same thing, so it differentiates nothing.

**"Experienced Plumbing Services"** - Anta, trust angle, scores 3 of 10.
The floor. No number, no proof, interchangeable with any plumber on earth. Useful only as calibration for what zero specificity looks like.

### Description lines

**"We are licensed with City of Toronto Municipal Licensing and Standards."** - New Canadian, trust angle, scores 8 of 10.
Names the actual issuing body, which is instantly more believable than the generic "licensed and insured" six competitors run.

**"Customers Have Said 'Excellent Results' 'Highly Recommend' 'Impressed' 'Spectacular Job!'"** - Birnie, trust angle, scores 7 of 10.
The only advertiser putting customers' actual words, not just stars, into ad copy. It reads like proof rather than a claim.

**"No Hidden Fees & Guaranteed Flat-Rate Pricing."** - Reliance, price angle, scores 7 of 10.
Two fee-fears killed in seven words. "Guaranteed" edges toward risk reversal, which is the closest anyone gets to the open lane.

**"Prevent Floods and Water Damages In your Basement. Get Up To $3,400 Government Rebate."** - New Canadian, outcome plus offer, scores 7 of 10.
Sells the disaster avoided, then pays for it with a rebate. Outcome framing nobody else attempts.

**"Fast 24-Hour Service In Southern Ontario. No Plumbing Job Too Big Or Small."** - Roto-Rooter, speed and capability, scores 6 of 10.
A serviceable stack, but both halves are shared claims. Table stakes executed cleanly.

**"Fast. Reliable. 20+ Years Of Experience. Same Day Service. Licensed & Insured. Call Today!"** - Priority, everything at once, scores 5 of 10.
Five angles machine-gunned into one line, so none of them lands. Real claims, zero focus.

**"Don't Drain Your Wallet."** - Mr. Rooter, price pun, scores 4 of 10.
Memorable, but it is second-read cleverness with no number behind it. The "instantly clear" trap in action.

**"Keep Your Pipes Flowing Smoothly With Our Drain Cleaning Experts."** - Anta, scores 3 of 10.
Smooth-sounding nothing. No number, no proof, no differentiation. This is what filler looks like.

### Sitelinks and extensions worth noting

**Promotion: "CA$100 off Drain Cleaning"** - Mr. Rooter, scores 8 of 10.
The biggest dollar figure in the market, and it rides a promotion extension so it costs no headline slot.

**Sitelink: "$25 Off Plumbing Today"** - Birnie, scores 7 of 10.
"Today" converts a static coupon into urgency, and it echoes the ad's offer so the whole block on the results page sells one thing.

**Local listing: 4.7 stars from 1,633 reviews** - New Canadian, scores 7 of 10.
The heaviest social proof on any results page here. It is an account asset rather than copy, which is exactly why it cannot be out-written.

**Sitelink: "Wet Basement Solutions"** - Birnie, scores 6 of 10.
Named after the symptom the homeowner is staring at rather than the service. Outcome-first labelling.

**Sitelink: "Emergency Toilet Service"** - Roto-Rooter, scores 6 of 10.
Hyper-specific emergency sub-service. It pre-qualifies the click far better than "Our Services" ever could.

**Sitelink: "Book an Appointment"** - Master Drain, scores 5 of 10.
The market's only booking-shaped call to action. Weak execution, but it marks the online-booking gap.

## What separates the 9s from the 4s

**A number a competitor cannot copy beats an adjective every time.** 100 years, $3,400, 1,633 reviews.

**One angle per line.** Priority's five-angle pile-up is the anti-pattern.

**The highest scores cluster exactly where the claim list thins out** - price fears, arrival time, real offers. That confirms the gaps above are worth owning.

**Nobody scored a 10 across all 79 ads.** Not one line is specific and differentiated and proof-backed at the same time. That is the standard our library has to hit.
