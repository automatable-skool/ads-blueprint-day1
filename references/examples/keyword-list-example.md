One campaign, because $2,000 a month cannot honestly fund three. Every service is an ad group inside it, sharing one budget and one bid strategy. A service earns its own campaign later, at about 30 conversions a month, through `/scale-account`.

Ad groups are single-theme service groups, never cities. Location is a campaign setting - whole service area, Presence. City goes in the ad via location insertion and on the page via a URL parameter.
