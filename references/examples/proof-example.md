**1. Never use a number or claim in an ad or page unless it is in this file.**

## Never say

**Unprovable superlatives:** "#1" · "best" · "best in town" · "best in the GTA" · "top-rated" · "lowest price" · "cheapest" · "unbeatable prices" · "most trusted" · "award-winning" beyond the specific awards listed further down.

**Competitors.** Never name one in any ad or on any page.

**Gas work.** This business is not TSSA gas-licensed and does not do gas. Never imply otherwise.

**Rounded-up numbers.** It is "12,000+ jobs", never "over 15,000". It is "4.9 stars", never "5 stars".

**An invented licence number.** Ontario has no blanket rule requiring a licence number in ads, but this business voluntarily prints "Licensed plumber #306A-48291" on landing pages for trust. Never alter or invent that number.
