StatBand is the proof band that sits between the hero and the services grid.

```jsx
<StatBand items={[{value:"14+",label:"years in the trade"},{value:"1,400+",label:"jobs completed"},{value:"4.9",label:"across 182 reviews",icon:"star"},{value:"24/7",label:"emergency service"}]} />
```

Three or four figures, each one true. Use StatBlock instead when the figures sit inside a card or column.
