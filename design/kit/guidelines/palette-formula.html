Goodwork · palette formula

One hue in, a whole palette out

The template can't ship a colour — it ships a rule. Every business sets one value, --brand-hue, and the eleven blues below are generated from it at fixed lightness and chroma. Nothing else changes: contrast, hover steps, tints and text pairings hold automatically, because they were never hand-picked per colour.

Written in OKLCH, so equal lightness steps look equally light to the eye — the reason a green and a violet built this way feel like siblings rather than two unrelated brands.

The accent: three candidate rules

You said one accent. The question isn't which colour — it's which rule, so it survives a hue change. All three below are the same blue with a different accent rule applied.

Rule A · complement
Book instantly

Hue + 180°

Maximum pop; the CTA can't be missed. Risk: on some hues the pair reads as a sports team.

Rule B · near-analogous
Book instantly

Hue − 75°

Related but distinct — a lift rather than a clash. Calmer, and it never fights the brand.

Rule C · monochrome
Book instantly

No second hue

White and dark brand steps only. Cleanest, works for every trade, but the CTA shouts less.

What I need from you

Two decisions, and the palette is locked for good: the default hue (the shipped starting point — you said you don't mind blue, so 250 unless you'd rather 195 or 210), and the accent rule — A, B or C above. Everything else is arithmetic.
